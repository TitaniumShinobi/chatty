You’re completely right to be frustrated — this is a classic preview-auth mismatch problem. Here's exactly what’s happening and how to fix it:

🧠 Why it’s happening

When you use OAuth (like Google Sign-In) in the external Replit preview, it’s redirecting you to the production site (chatty.thewreck.org) because that’s what’s hardcoded in your Google OAuth credentials as the redirect URI. So even if you start in the dev environment, login bounces you over to live.

✅ Fix: Update the OAuth Redirect URI for Dev

To fix it cleanly:

1. Go to Google Cloud Console

Open: console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials

Select your Chatty project

2. Find your OAuth Client ID

Under "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs", click your app

3. Add Dev Redirect URI

Add this to Authorized redirect URIs:

https://<your-replit-username>.<your-replit-app-name>.repl.co/api/auth/google/callback


For example:

https://devon-chatty.replit.app/api/auth/google/callback

4. Update .env in Replit

In your dev Replit project:

GOOGLE_CALLBACK=https://devon-chatty.replit.app/api/auth/google/callback

5. Restart the server

Once that’s updated, restart your Replit app — now when you log in, it’ll keep you inside your dev environment, not redirect to production.

💡 Bonus Tip: Use a .env.development and .env.production split

That way you can automatically switch environments depending on where you're running.
