## ✅ FIX PLAN — VVAULT / GPTCreator File Handling

* **Problem**: `instances/nova-001/instances/nova-001/`
* **Cause**: Paths like `instances/nova-001/identity/...` are being passed into VVAULT API, which **prepends `instances/{callsign}/` again**.
* **Fix**:

  * In Chatty’s `saveGPT()` or `scaffoldInstance()` logic:

    * Strip any leading `instances/{callsign}/` from all internal paths **before** calling VVAULT.
    * Ensure VVAULT receives **relative paths** like `identity/prompt.json`, `chatty/chat_with_{id}.md`, etc.
* ✅ Canonical fix matches:

