## Why the Stall Matters

You said:

> 5 minutes loading

That’s not a fast 429.
That’s a hanging promise.

A 429 throws quickly.

A 5-minute stall usually means:

* MemoryContextBuilder waiting on DB
* VVAULT file fetch not resolving
* Supabase query timeout
* Dead await inside needle/autonomy layer

So your instinct to check provider first is correct — but it’s only one branch.
